      01 · Who We Are

      Occupy
      till I come.

      Christ Extension Church Int'l is a global ministry. Our divine mandate is to bring holistic transformation to God's people through sound, inspiring and revelatory teaching from God's holy and infallible Word.

      We want God's people to live productive, purposeful lives here on earth, aware that what we do here counts in the age to come. We act on our Lord's words in Luke 19:13. Our meetings equip and empower people to be relevant in every sphere of life, with Kingdom influence that lasts for generations, for God's glory.

      Founded byPastor Emmanuel & Sheila Mbai
      Our Mission

      Raising a unique breed of people with the utmost value for God's presence, who have come to see and appreciate that everything about life begins, thrives and culminates in His presence.

      Our Vision

      To become the church that Christ envisioned while on the cross, allowing Him to fully function, manifest Himself and prosper His pleasure through us here on earth.

      04 · Clear Space & Size

      Room to
      breathe.

      Keep text, images and page edges out of the space around the logo. The unit of measure is x, the height of the capital “E” in Extension.

      Clear space
      At least 1x on every side, which is about 7% of the logo's width.
      Rule of thumb
      If in doubt, give it more space.

      06 · The Mark

      The cross
      and its reach.

      The mark is the “t” from the logo on its own: the cross, with its stem turning and running out into a long tail. It says Christ, extended without a single word.

      Use it where the church is already named nearby, such as a profile, a stage screen or merchandise, and as the signature graphic that ties all our materials together.

      Construction

      Every part is measured in T, the thickness of the stroke. The crossbar is centred on the stem. The tail has no fixed length. It reaches as far as the layout allows.

      Red on white: primary
      White on Cross Red
      White on Ink Black

      Square spaces

      Profile pictures, browser tabs and app icons are square, so the full tail can't fit. In these spaces only, the tail stops in line with the end of the crossbar.

      07 · The Extension Line

      Words that stand
      on the line.

      In the logo, “Extension Church” stands on the line that runs out from the cross. We use the same move everywhere: a title, a name or a date standing on the extension line, with the line running out to the edge. You've seen it at the top of every chapter in this book.

      Headlines: the title stands on the line, and the line runs to the edge.

      Prolonging
      His Days

      Names and captions: speakers, ministries and events.

      Youth MinistryFridays · 6 pm

      Closing lines: the last thing on a post, slide or page.

      Because I am here,
      He is here.

      Always from the cross

      The line always starts at the cross. It is never a loose underline on its own.

      Always to the edge

      Let the tail run off the page or screen. Don't stop it halfway or curve it.

      One per layout

      Use it once per page, slide or post. Used more often, it stops meaning anything.

      11 · Voice & Tone

      Words that
      welcome.

      We speak the way a good host greets a guest at the door: warmly and plainly, and rooted in the Word. We always capitalise He, His and Him when we refer to God.

      Welcoming

      Write for the first-time visitor. Explain, don't assume.

      Not cliquey or full of jargon

      Revelatory

      Teach from the Word, and let scripture carry the weight.

      Not preachy or vague

      Purposeful

      Point to productive, Kingdom-minded living, now and for eternity.

      Not fearful or guilt-driven

      Clear

      Short sentences. Time, place and next step, every time.

      Not wordy
      We say
      • “You're welcome here, just as you are.”
      • “Join us Sunday at 10 am. Parking is behind the hall.”
      • “What we do here counts in the age to come.”
      We avoid
      • “All members are hereby reminded that attendance is mandatory.”
      • “Don't miss out or you'll regret it!!!”
      • “Pre-service intercessory convocation, 0900 hrs.”
      Our name
      “Christ Extension Church Int'l” in formal documents, and “Christ Extension Church” everywhere else. Write it in full the first time and use “CEC” after that, but never on its own in a headline.
      Our tagline
      “Prospering His pleasure on earth”, from Isaiah 53:10, with a capital H and no full stop.
      Capitals
      Sentence case for headings and buttons. Capitals only for short labels.

      13 · On Screen & Outdoor

      From the phone
      to the roadside.

      On screen and outdoors, the same system holds: warm parchment space, Garamond headlines, Cross Red reserved for the one thing to do next, and the cross leading the eye.

      App
      Use Parchment backgrounds and Garamond headlines. Cross Red is for primary buttons, the active tab and play controls only, so there's one red action per screen.
      Billboards
      Keep the headline to five words or fewer, readable in five seconds. Use the logo at full size, with the extension line starting from the cross.
      Imagery
      Use warm, natural light. Real people worshipping, or the lamb as a symbol of the offering in Isaiah 53. Never cold, dark or staged.
      One red action

      Each app screen gets one red button or control. Everything else stays ink and parchment.

      The line from the cross

      On the “Rooted” billboard, the headline stands on a line that starts at the cross. That is the extension line used correctly.

      No loose rules

      A red line that doesn't start from the cross, like the one under the “Welcome” billboard, should be removed before printing.

      14 · Merchandise

      Carried into
      the world.

      Every mug, cap and lanyard carries the name a little further. Apply the logo with the same care as on paper: full colour on light materials, all white on red and black.

      Printing
      Use the vector files (SVG or PDF) from Downloads, and ask suppliers for a printed proof matched to Cross Red.
      Embroidery
      On caps and polos, keep the logo at least 60 mm wide so the tagline stitches cleanly. Narrower than that, use the logo without the tagline.
      Small items
      For pins, buttons and charms, use the mark alone.

      Click any photo to enlarge it. These are concept visuals. Check every production proof against these guidelines.
